How added fields are calculated

These are calculated examples, not questionnaire answers. Sleep quality is modeled sleep score divided by 20, rounded to 1 to 5. Before-bed minutes use screen hours and modeled night-use share (max 120); wakeups use night-use share divided by 12 (max 7); notifications are pickups multiplied by 3.2. Reduction minutes use the modeled reduction index and screen minutes; mood and focus use the modeled wellbeing index. Activity and AI-task groups are inferred from platform and modeled AI sessions.
